— Why Designers Work With Us

Your reputation ships
with every piece.

A client does not see the thirty vendors, the six-month lead times, or the freight carrier that left a crate in the rain. They see one room, once, on the day you hand it over. Everything that happens before that moment is invisible — unless it goes wrong.

Most logistics companies treat a design project like a household move. They do not read floor plans, they do not understand installation sequence, and they do not grasp what it means when a custom sofa with a twenty-week lead time arrives gouged on the morning of a reveal.

We built this operation around the way design projects actually run. You keep the client relationship and the design decisions. We take everything between the purchase order and the finished room.

— Where It Usually Goes Wrong

The six moments
that cost you.

01

The vendor list is thirty deep

Thirty vendors on thirty timelines, none of them talking to each other. We become the single delivery address for all of them, then consolidate everything into one coordinated installation on your date.

02

The job site is not ready

Millwork slips, the GC needs another three weeks, and freight is already in transit. Pieces come to us instead and wait in climate-controlled storage for as long as the project needs.

03

A crate arrives damaged

Found at our facility, it is a vendor claim we file for you while the freight window is still open. Found on install day, it is your problem with the client standing in the room.

04

The building has rules

COIs, elevator reservations, restricted delivery hours, an island address with a bridge schedule. We handle building requirements as part of onboarding, not as a surprise on the morning of.

05

Install day is the reveal

Floors and walls protected, pieces placed to your spec, art hung to your heights, packaging removed. We walk the rooms with you and do not close out until you sign off.

06

The client changes their mind

A piece goes back, a replacement comes in, a room gets rethought after the fact. Returns, vendor coordination, and follow-on deliveries run through the same coordinator who knows the project.

— Working Together

From first PO
to punch list.

01

We learn the project

You share the vendor list, the job site, and your target install window. We set up a receiving address tied to your project, open your inventory record, and brief your vendors on where to ship. From that point they stop calling you about delivery windows.

02

Your vendors ship to us

Freight carriers, parcel, white-glove carriers, fine art shippers, international forwarders. Everything is signed for and logged against your project. You get a notification as each piece lands.

03

You see it before the job site does

Every piece unwrapped, inspected under proper lighting, and photographed. A condition report reaches you within 48 hours. If something arrived wrong, you know while there is still time to fix it — and we file the claim.

04

We hold until you say go

Re-wrapped and held in climate-controlled storage. Your timeline can move by a week or by six months and nothing about the plan changes. Storage bills monthly, so a slipped schedule is not a penalty.

05

We install to your spec

Staged by room and sequence, delivered in one coordinated run. Protection down before anything comes through the door. Furniture placed, art hung, soft goods dressed, debris removed — then a room-by-room walkthrough with you or your project manager.

06

We stay through punch list

Returns, replacements, outstanding vendor claims, and the pieces that arrive after the reveal. Same coordinator, same standard, until the project is genuinely closed.
