— What Designer Services Means

The logistics layer
designers rely on.

Most logistics companies treat a design project like a move. They don't understand floor plans, installation sequences, or what it means when a piece arrives damaged the morning of a client reveal. Safebound Luxury was built for the way designers actually work.

We operate as your logistics department — accepting your vendors, coordinating your schedule, managing your inventory, and executing your installation. You make the design decisions. We handle everything that has to happen before the client walks in.

From a single receiving job to a full estate FF&E rollout, every engagement gets a dedicated coordinator, a documented inventory, and a team that knows your project before they touch a single piece.

project requires.

— How It Works

From first vendor
to client reveal.

01

Project Kickoff

We assign a dedicated coordinator to your project. You share your vendor list, project address, and installation target. We set up your receiving address, inventory portal, and project timeline — and brief your vendors so they know exactly where to ship.

02

Vendor Shipments Received

Your vendors ship to our facility. We accept every delivery, sign for every piece, and log everything into your project inventory. You get a notification when each item arrives and a condition report within 48 hours.

03

Inspection & Damage Documentation

Every piece is unwrapped, inspected, and photographed before storage. Damage is documented at the point of receipt — not on install day. Vendor claims are filed immediately while the evidence is valid and fresh.

04

Climate-Controlled Hold

Inspected pieces are re-wrapped and stored in our 80,000 sq. ft. climate-controlled facility. Your inventory dashboard shows real-time status — arrived, pending, flagged, or cleared for delivery — until your project is ready.

05

Staged Delivery & Installation

When your schedule is ready, we stage everything by room and floor, deliver in installation sequence, place every piece per your spec, hang art, remove debris, and do a room-by-room walkthrough. We don't leave until you approve.

06

Post-Install Support

Returns, replacements, vendor claims, and follow-on deliveries handled by the same coordinator. Your project doesn't close until every piece is right and every outstanding item is resolved.
