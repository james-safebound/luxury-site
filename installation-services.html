— What Installation Services Means

The final step
done right.

Installation is where a project becomes real — and where it can go wrong. A single scratch on a custom sofa, a piece hung an inch off-center, furniture placed without regard for the designer's intent — these aren't small mistakes on a luxury project. They're costly ones.

Safebound Luxury's installation team operates as an extension of your design team. We study your floor plan, follow your placement guide, protect every surface in the home, and work until the last piece is exactly where it belongs.

From single-room furniture placement to full estate installations spanning multiple floors and hundreds of pieces, we run the process — so you don't have to.

◈

Furniture Placement & Assembly

Every piece unwrapped, assembled where required, and placed precisely per your floor plan. Blankets and runners protect floors and walls throughout.

◈

Art Hanging & Gallery Installation

Trained art handlers hang pieces at the correct height, angle, and hardware for each wall type. We work from your spec sheet or coordinate directly with your designer.

◈

Debris & Packing Removal

◈

— How It Works

From schedule to
finished room.

01

Pre-Installation Planning

We review your floor plan, placement guide, and room-by-room spec sheet before a single piece leaves the warehouse. Our team walks the job site when possible, confirms access, elevator reservations, and protection requirements — so install day has no surprises.

02

Surface Protection Setup

Before any furniture enters the home, our team lays floor runners, corner guards, and wall protection throughout the delivery path and in every installation room. Nothing moves until the property is fully protected.

03

Staged Delivery by Room

Pieces arrive in installation sequence — organized by room and floor. We don't unload everything and sort it out inside. Each delivery run brings exactly what the next room needs, keeping the home clean and organized throughout.

04

Placement, Assembly & Hanging

Furniture is unwrapped, assembled where needed, and placed per your specification. Art and mirrors are hung at the correct height and angle. Soft goods are dressed and styled. Every piece is handled by the same trained specialists from start to finish.

05

Room-by-Room Sign-Off

Before moving to the next room, our project lead does a final walkthrough with your designer or representative. Any adjustments are made on the spot. Nothing is closed out until you approve it.

06

Debris Removal & Final Walkthrough

All packing materials, wrapping, and debris are removed from the property. Our team does a final pass of every room, protections are removed, and we leave the home exactly as the designer intends it to look.
