— The Work

Pieces that
demand more.

Not everything fits a standard service description. This is a curated record of the specialized projects, unique pieces, and unconventional engagements that define what Safebound Luxury actually does at its highest level.

Each entry represents a real project — the kind that requires judgment, expertise, and a team that knows when to call in the right specialist.

Fine Art

Large-Format Canvas, Palm Beach Estate

An 11-foot contemporary canvas received from a New York gallery, custom crated, climate-transported, and installed over a double-height foyer staircase using engineered rigging. Museum-grade hardware, single attempt.

Furniture

1,000-Pound Marble Dining Tables

A pair of custom Italian marble dining tables — each exceeding 500 lbs — received, stored, and installed in a Boca Raton estate. Engineered floor protection, specialized dollies, and a four-man placement team.

Custom Crating

Sculptural Bronze Collection

Fourteen bronze sculptures from a private collector's estate, each requiring bespoke foam-lined crating to accommodate irregular forms. Transported to climate storage ahead of a gallery loan.

Installation

Full Estate FF&E Install, Wellington

Complete furniture and fixture installation across a 14,000 sq. ft. equestrian estate — 200+ pieces, 12 rooms, coordinated over four days from our receiving facility to final room staging.

Yacht & Marine

Superyacht Interior Refit, Fort Lauderdale

Custom furniture and bespoke fixtures received and staged for a 160-foot superyacht interior refit at Fort Lauderdale marina. Dock-side delivery, gangway coordination, and onboard placement by our specialist team.

Hospitality

Boutique Hotel Opening, Palm Beach

34-room boutique hotel FF&E rollout — all furniture, fixtures, and artwork received, inspected, and installed across six floors in under two weeks. Phased by floor, signed off room by room.

Fine Art

Antique Map Collection, Private Gallery

Forty-two antique maps — some dating to the 17th century — received from an auction house, individually sleeved, climate-stored, and installed in a purpose-built private gallery with archival hanging systems.

Estate

Multi-Property Estate Transition, Palm Beach Island

A family office managing transition of contents across three Palm Beach Island properties — full inventory, climate storage, and staged re-installation as renovations completed on each property over eight months.

Custom Crating

Grand Piano, Ocean-View Penthouse

A 9-foot Steinway concert grand transported to a 34th-floor penthouse with no freight elevator large enough. Coordinated crane lift, custom rigging, and a post-transit tuning specialist — delivered in one day.
